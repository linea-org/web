Starting a graph is the easy half. Cron, a webhook, and `client.run()` are solved problems.

The hard half is the run that crashes on step four, the fact that has to live until next Tuesday, and the one node you need to call again without paying for steps one through three.

Linea takes the trigger and then does the work those triggers do not cover.
